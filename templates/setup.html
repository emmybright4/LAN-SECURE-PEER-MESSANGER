Welcome aboard

This app runs entirely on your local network. No internet connection, no subscription and no data leaving your machines — just encrypted chat between the devices on the same LAN.

  • No internet connection required
  • Nothing is sent to external servers
  • Messages stay on your local network
  • You control the encryption level
  • No email or phone number required
